About Us

What Sagar Mitra Actually Does

Beneath every wave is a story most people never get to read. Sagar Mitra listens to it through a fleet of autonomous ARGO floats — free-drifting probes that sink to nearly 2,000 metres, ride the deep currents for ten days, then rise slowly back to the surface. On the way up, onboard sensors log pressure, temperature, and salinity at every metre and beam the finished profile home the moment the float breaks water. We run that raw signal through the TEOS-10 standard and a detection engine tuned to catch marine heatwaves, oxygen minimum zones, and hidden heat traps below the surface — turning a wall of numbers into something a fisherman, a student, or a researcher can actually use.
